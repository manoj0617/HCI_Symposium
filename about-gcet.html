About GCET

Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology, Cheeryal, Hyderabad.

Overview

Where the symposium happens

Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology opened in 2005 in Cheeryal, on the north-eastern edge of Hyderabad. It is an autonomous college affiliated to JNTU Hyderabad, run by the Teja Educational Society, and holds an A+ grade from NAAC.

Students here study B.Tech, M.Tech and MBA programmes, with computer science branches in AI & ML, data science, cyber security and IoT alongside electronics, electrical, civil and mechanical engineering. Project work, industry links and a campus incubation centre sit next to the classroom, under the college motto "Striving towards perfection."
