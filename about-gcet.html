Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology

College overview

Established in 2005, Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology is an autonomous engineering college in Cheeryal, Hyderabad, affiliated to Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH). It is run by the Teja Educational Society as part of the Geethanjali Group of Institutions, and is accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade.

GCET offers B.Tech, M.Tech and MBA programmes across computer science (including AI & ML, data science, cyber security and IoT), electronics, electrical, civil and mechanical engineering. Its education links coursework with interdisciplinary projects, industry interaction and entrepreneurship, guided by the motto "Striving towards perfection."

Major achievements

  • Ranked 77th among the top 175 engineering institutes by Times Engineering / I3RC (2024–25).
  • Ranked 6th in Telangana and 5th in the Category of Excellence by GHRDC (2024–25).
  • Ranked 25th among private engineering colleges in Telangana by The Week (2024–25).
  • Rated AAA+ among India's best engineering colleges by Careers360.
  • Recognised as an R&D centre (SIRO) by DSIR, Government of India, in 2011.
  • Received the Engineering Educators of the Year award from Bradford University, UK and Education Matters in 2014.
  • Runs an AICTE IDEA Lab, an Institution's Innovation Council and an Incubation Centre, and has MoUs with the University of Illinois Springfield (USA) and Tech Mahindra.
