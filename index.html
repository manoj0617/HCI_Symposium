HCIPAI Education Initiative · Faculty only

Integrating HCI/HMI Design with Engineering Curricula

A participatory workshop

How do we prepare engineers not just to build technology, but to solve the right problems and create sustainable value for humanity?

Engineering education has traditionally emphasised technical knowledge and domain-specific skills. Yet an AI-driven world demands more: critical thinking, systems understanding, human awareness, ethical judgement, and the ability to address complex, real-world problems. Integrating Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) and Human–Machine Interaction (HMI) offers an opportunity to strengthen these capabilities within engineering education.

Why now

NEP 2020 calls for multidisciplinary learning, critical thinking, problem-solving, experiential learning, and applying knowledge to real-world challenges. Evolving job profiles demand engineers who can question assumptions, understand contexts, anticipate consequences, and develop responsible, sustainable solutions — moving beyond a predominantly theory-based pedagogy without compromising the rigour of engineering disciplines.

In the workshop, we will

  1. Explore where HCI/HMI can add value to each engineering discipline.
  2. Identify the capabilities future-ready engineering graduates need.
  3. Validate and refine a proposed curriculum framework for adoption.
  4. Examine relevant pedagogies and approaches to curriculum integration.

The aim is not to add another subject to an already demanding curriculum, but to explore how engineering education can better cultivate thoughtful, capable, and responsible problem-solvers.
