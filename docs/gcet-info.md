# GCET — reference facts for the About page

Collected from gcet.edu.in on 2026-10-18. Get the organisers to confirm anything marked ⚠ before launch.

## Identity
- **Full name:** Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), part of the Geethanjali Group of Institutions
- **Society:** Teja Educational Society
- **Motto:** "Striving towards perfection"
- **Status:** Autonomous, with UGC 2(f) and 12(b) status (2016)
- **Established:** 2005 (confirmed by the team)
- **Affiliation:** JNTU Hyderabad (JNTUH) (confirmed by the team)

## Address and contact
- Sy. No. 33 & 34, Cheeryala (V), Keesara (M), Medchal Dist., Telangana 501301 (from the official site; Google Maps shows 501303)
- +91 98663 08271 · info@gcet.edu.in

## Accreditations
- **NAAC:** 'A' grade in 2017, re-accredited 'A+' in 2022
- **NBA:**
  - First accredited in 2012 (CSE, ECE, IT)
  - Re-accredited in 2015, 2018 and 2022
  - B.Tech CE accredited in 2021
  - **CSE, ECE and EEE re-accredited under NBA Tier-1 in 2025**
- DSIR recognised it as an R&D centre (SIRO) in 2011

## Other figures
- 100+ faculty with a Ph.D.
- 1,450+ placements in 2025
- Recruiters include Amazon, IBM, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, Virtusa, Genpact, NTT Data and others
- Built-up area: 40,773 sq.m
- 2,926 computers, 2 Gbps internet available 24 hours
- MoUs with the University of Illinois Springfield (USA) and Tech Mahindra. GCET is also part of Tech Mahindra's Campus Connect programme.
- Engineering Educators of the Year award (Bradford University, UK and Education Matters, 2014)
- Innovation: AICTE IDEA Lab, Institution's Innovation Council, Incubation Centre, Geenovate Foundation
